# Jumia Product Performance Analysis

## Overview
This project analyzes Jumia product data to identify top-performing and underperforming products based on ratings, reviews, and discounts.

## Objectives
- Analyze product ratings and customer reviews.
- Identify top and bottom performing products.
- Evaluate discount trends and their impact on performance.
- Detect missing values and data quality issues.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Dashboard Visualizations

## Key Insights
- Top 5 highest-rated products identified.
- Bottom 5 lowest-rated products highlighted.
- Top 10 products by reviews analyzed.
- Top 10 discounted products with strong ratings identified.
- Missing data assessed for quality improvement.

## Files Included
- **Dashboard** – Interactive summary of findings.
- **Pivot Tables** – Supporting analysis.
- **Dataset** – Raw and cleaned product data.
- **Top/Bottom Product Analysis** – Ratings, reviews, and discount insights.

## Conclusion
The analysis provides insights into customer preferences, product performance, and discount effectiveness, helping support data-driven business decisions.
